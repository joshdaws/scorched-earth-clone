Privacy Policy

Scorched Earth: Synthwave

Scorched Earth: Synthwave is designed to be playable without an account. Core game progress, settings, tank unlocks, supply drop state, achievements, and local scores are stored on your device.

Information We Collect

The current app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information. It stores gameplay preferences and progress locally using browser or WebView storage.

Online Services

Online leaderboard or high-score services may be enabled for some builds. If enabled, the app may send gameplay score data, display name, run statistics, and basic technical request information needed to operate the service. Gameplay remains available when those services are offline.

Ads, Tracking, And Purchases

This build does not include advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or in-app purchase SDKs. If those features are added later, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure must be updated before release.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. If online score services are enabled, data is shared only with the service providers needed to operate app functionality.
